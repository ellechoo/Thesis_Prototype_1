How to read the result

The model was trained on volunteer recordings from Mozilla Common Voice. It sorts speech into 16 broad groups, such as “United States” or “England”. It can't tell regions within a country apart, and it has no group for many accents, so a mixed or regional accent gets matched to whichever group sounds most similar.

The percentages show how much the model leans toward each group. They aren't a measure of how “strong” an accent is. Background noise, a far-away mic or very short recordings make results less reliable. Try reading the passage twice and compare.

Accents say nothing about ability or intelligence. Treat this as a curiosity, not a judgment about a person.
